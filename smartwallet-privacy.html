SmartWallet HK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026 · Contact: singhramandeep0910@gmail.com

SmartWallet HK is an offline personal finance app for Hong Kong. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Summary

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. All financial information you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in an on-device SQLite database:

  • Transactions, categories, budgets, and recurring rules you create
  • App preferences such as language choice and onboarding status

This data is not uploaded to our servers because the app does not use cloud sync or user accounts.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No location tracking
  • No contacts, photos, or microphone access

CSV export

If you use CSV export, the file is saved to your device storage. You control where that file goes afterward.

Third-party services

SmartWallet HK does not require an internet connection for core features and does not send your ledger data to third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
